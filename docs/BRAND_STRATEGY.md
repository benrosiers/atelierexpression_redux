# Brand Strategy — Atelier Expression

## Identity Statement

Atelier Expression is a joyful, safe, human workshop space for adults who have learned to make themselves small. We offer structured play, laughter, movement, voice, and connection for people who feel something buried in them and want to find their way back to it.

**This is not therapy. This is not coaching. This is not art school. This is permission.**

---

## Name

**Atelier Expression**
- "Atelier" = workshop, studio, laboratory. It suggests creative work, intentional space, skilled facilitation. Grounded. Not ethereal.
- "Expression" = the full human expression. Voice, body, emotion, laughter, presence. Not art, not performance — expression.
- Together: a workshop for expression. Professional, accessible, bilingual-friendly.

---

## Tagline Hierarchy

**Primary:** On rit. On joue. On s'exprime.
- Three short clauses. Declarative. Not promises — descriptions.
- Rhythm matters. It reads like a manifesto.

**Secondary:** Des ateliers pour adultes qui veulent se sentir plus vivants.
- Positions the offer clearly. Adults. Feeling alive. Not "development." Not "growth." **Alive.**

**Tertiary phrases:**
- Prendre sa place, avec joie.
- Exprimer sans se rapetisser.
- Bouger. Rire. Créer. Oser.
- Aucun talent requis. Aucune performance. Juste un espace pour essayer.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Headlines | Playfair Display | Editorial, expressive, confident. Grown-up but alive. |
| Body/UI | DM Sans | Clean, warm, accessible. Contemporary. |
| Accent (sparingly) | Caveat | Handwritten. Used ONLY for short accent phrases like "pour adultes vivants" — never for body copy. |

**Typography rules:**
- Serif headlines drive emotion.
- Sans body drives clarity.
- Handwritten accents create human warmth — use max 1–2 per page.
- Never use handwritten font for sentences longer than 6 words.
- Fluid type sizes using `clamp()` for responsive scale.

## Visual Marks / Doodles

**Allowed marks (SVG, CSS):**
- Stars (✦ ✹) — burst, energy, joy
- Circles/rings — connection, group, cycle
- Zigzags — laughter, voice waves
- Dots — movement, trajectory, bubbles
- Irregular blobs — organic, human, imperfect

**Rules:**
- Use sparingly. Never more than 2–3 per page.
- Animate gently (float, spin-slow) — respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Never decorate for the sake of decoration. Each mark should reinforce an emotion.

## Photography Direction

**What we show:**
- Groups of adults (5–10) laughing together
- Movement — hands, open bodies, imperfect gestures
- Candid moments — not staged smiles
- Warm ambient light (natural preferred)
- Circles of people sharing
- Cindy as facilitator — laughing WITH people, not AT them

**What we never show:**
- Solo beauty portraits
- Seductive framing or lighting
- Dark, dramatic, cabaret atmosphere
- Yoga/meditation aesthetic
- Corporate handshakes or boardroom staging
- Revealing clothing

**Color treatment:**
- Natural warmth preferred. Can add warm overlay if photos feel cold.
- Photos sit in organic shapes (arched top, blob, rounded rectangle) to feel editorial.

---

## Tone of Voice

**Persona:** Smart, warm, funny-but-not-silly. Honest. Slightly punkette. Not a guru. Not a coach. Not a therapist. A person who loves people and creates spaces for them.

**Rules:**
- Tutoiement throughout ("tu", "ton", "toi")
- Short sentences preferred
- No fake urgency ("RÉSERVE MAINTENANT!!!")
- No therapy language ("guérir", "trauma", "chakra", "sacré")
- No coaching jargon ("mindset", "breakthrough", "empowerment")
- No shame, no judgment, no "you should" energy
- Humor is allowed — gentle, self-aware, never at the expense of the audience
- Acknowledge fear and hesitation directly — don't pretend they don't exist

**Forbidden words/phrases:**
guérir · trauma · sacré · chakra · énergie féminine · thérapie · coaching · sensualité · développement personnel (as generic catch-all) · performance · spectacle · héroïque

## Visual Hierarchy Per Page

1. Emotional headline (serif, large)
2. Clarifying subtitle (sans, medium)
3. Handwritten accent (small, one per section max)
4. Body copy (sans, comfortable line-length)
5. CTA (always rounded pill, fuchsia primary)
6. Accent marks (subtle, never dominant)

---

## Anti-Drift Rules

This brand must NOT drift toward:
- Beige/neutral wellness aesthetic
- Spiritual/sacred feminine energy
- Therapy/healing language
- Glamour/cabaret/adult entertainment coding
- Corporate coaching tone
- Generic self-help inspirational quotes
- Giant hero portraits of Cindy
- Any framing that centers a single personality over the community

This brand MUST maintain:
- Joy first
- Group before individual
- Human before brand
- Play before performance
- Warmth with intelligence
